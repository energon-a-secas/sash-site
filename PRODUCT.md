# Sash: product one-pager

**What it is:** Collect badges and certificates on one profile, claim them from expiring links, and share it as an image or an embed

**Who it is for:** Somebody whose real credential wallet is a wall of logos they
did not choose, half of them expired, none of them about the work their team
actually values. They want a page for the badges that mean something to the
people around them: the on-call save, the review nobody else would have caught,
the running joke that became a title.

**Register:** product (a tool someone uses), not brand (a page someone visits).

**The scene:** Ten minutes after a release, on a laptop, somebody pastes a claim
link into a team channel. Everyone opens it, signs in, and the badge is on their
profile before the retrospective starts. Later, alone, one of them opens their
own wall and spends two minutes deciding what sits at the front of it.

**What done looks like:** The wall reads the way its owner wants it to. The
badges they are proud of are first, in an order they chose; the ones they are
not are hidden; and the picture they downloaded says who issued each badge and
where to check it, so sharing it costs them nothing to explain.
